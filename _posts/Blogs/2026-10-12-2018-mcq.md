# 2018 Practice Exam MCQ Reflection

## My Quiz Results

![2018 Practice Exam MCQ results overview]({{ site.baseurl }}/images/2018-mcq-results-overview.png)

- My actual score was probably closer to **20/66 (about 30%)**. The screenshot shows **61/66** because I went back and checked my work as working through questions without fully reading the problems. That was not my actual score.
- After finding out the answers, I found that a lot of questions made more sense to me and I could connect the dots on how to solve them in a space where I normally would be clueless. Figuring out answers to previous questions improved my ability to solve later questions.

## My Analytics

- These percentages are from after I reviewed my answers, so they don't show exactly what I knew on my first try.
- Still, I saw I had some strengths and weaknesses because I did not start correcting answers until about question 20.

## What I Felt Better About

- **Binary Numbers (100%):** This was one of the categories I did better in after reviewing the quiz. I feel like I have a better idea of how binary works now.
- **Working with Data (100%):** I got 100% in both Data Compression and Extracting Information from Data after checking my work. These topics made more sense to me while going over my answers.

## What I Need to Work On

- **Conditionals and Random Values (50% each):** These were my lowest categories. I need more practice following `if` and `else` statements and figuring out what values a program could produce.
- **Calling Procedures (60%) and Identifying and Correcting Errors (75%):** These were also near the bottom. I still have trouble keeping track of what code is doing when a procedure runs and catching mistakes without help.
- Longer questions were hard for me to stay focused and locked in to solve as well.

## What I need to improve on in classroom

- I think the main goal for me needs to be working more with my teammates, they know a lot more than me and are a good resource and collaboraters
- I also need to try and stay on top of work more and not get behind, in the past, I was very late to submit assignments and by doing this I think my knowledge and grade will improve an answer once I see it is a lot easier than figuring it out on my own.
